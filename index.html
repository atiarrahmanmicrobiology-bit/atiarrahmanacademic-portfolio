01 / ABOUT

Curious about
microbial life.

I am Md. Atiar Rahman, a Microbiology graduate from Jagannath University, Bangladesh, with research interests spanning environmental microbiology, microalgae-based wastewater treatment, and bacterial genomics.

My academic work has focused on understanding microbial communities in fish-market wastewater and exploring the potential of microalgae for wastewater bioremediation. I am also interested in the genomic features, antimicrobial resistance determinants, and virulence-associated traits of bacterial isolates.
